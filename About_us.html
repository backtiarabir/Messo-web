About Messo

Messo is a platform that helps students discover trusted and reliable accommodation across the country. We connect students with verified hostels and landlords to create a safe, convenient and affordable living experience.

Our goal is to simplify the housing search so students can focus on learning and personal growth.

Our Vision

To be the leading student housing marketplace that delivers safe, comfortable and affordable homes for every student.

Our Mission

To simplify the accommodation journey by offering verified listings, transparent pricing and helpful tools that empower students to make confident decisions.

Core Values

  • Student-first: We design for students' needs.
  • Trust: We verify and monitor listings for safety and quality.
  • Transparency: Clear information and fair pricing.
  • Continuous improvement: We iterate based on feedback and data.
